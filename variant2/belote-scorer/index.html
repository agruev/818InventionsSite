Belote Scorer

Keep score in Belote without pen and paper.

Screenshot coming soon

What it does

Belote Scorer replaces the scribbled napkin at the card table. Enter each round in a few taps and the app adds up the points, tracks declarations and tells you who's ahead, so you can focus on the game instead of the math.

Features

  • Fast round entryLog a round in seconds, right after the last trick.
  • Automatic totalsPoints, declarations and belote are summed for you.
  • Game historyLook back at every round if someone disputes the score.
  • Works offlineNo connection needed at the table.
