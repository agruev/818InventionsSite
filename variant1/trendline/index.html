Trendline

Track your weight and see the real trend, not the daily noise.

What it does

Your weight jumps up and down every day for reasons that have nothing to do with progress. Trendline smooths out that noise and shows the line that actually matters, so a bad morning on the scale doesn't ruin your week.

Features

  • Quick daily loggingEnter today's weight in one step.
  • Smoothed trendSee where you're really heading, not just yesterday's number.
  • Goal trackingSet a target and watch the distance shrink.
  • Runs in the browserNothing to install, works on any device.
