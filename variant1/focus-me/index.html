Focus Me

A virtual camera that keeps you sharp and in frame on every video call.

Screenshot coming soon

What it does

Focus Me sits between your webcam and your video call app. It shows up as a regular camera, so you just pick it in Zoom, Teams or Meet and it takes care of keeping you looking good on screen.

Features

  • Works everywhereAppears as a normal webcam in any video app.
  • Keeps you in frameFollows you when you move, so you stay centered.
  • Sharp focusPuts the attention on you, not on the room behind you.
  • LightweightRuns quietly in the background on Windows.

Available for Windows.
